The bundled calibration (v0.4.1)
================================

You do NOT need these files for a fresh install: the mod's built-in
defaults are identical to them. Install the two DLLs, press VR PRESET 1,
play.

They matter only if you ALREADY have your own tuning in
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\BioshockVR\
(your files always win over the built-in defaults, key by key):

- Keep your tuning:        do nothing.
- Adopt this calibration:  back up and replace vrpreset.ini, hands.ini
                           and weapons.ini in %LOCALAPPDATA%\BioshockVR\
                           with these, then restart the game.
- Mix and match:           copy only the file you want (e.g. weapons.ini
                           for the per-weapon aim profiles), or even
                           single key=value lines between files - every
                           line stands alone.
