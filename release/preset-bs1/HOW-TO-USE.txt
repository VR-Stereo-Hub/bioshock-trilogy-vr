The bundled calibration (2026-10-24, the developer's headset tune)
==================================================================

These are the developer's own settings from the build this release was
tested with: the hand and arm tune (hands.ini), the per-weapon and
per-plasmid aim profiles (weapons.ini), the VR preset (vrpreset.ini),
every F10 menu choice (menu-settings.ini: hand and weapon size, the
grip calibration, HUD and screen placement, upscaler and stereo method)
and the controller bindings (BioshockVR.ini). The mod's built-in
defaults are older than some of these.

The hand calibration is tuned to one pair of Quest 3 controllers in one
person's hands. If your hands sit wrong with it, re-tune in F10 (Hands)
or delete hands.ini and the HandCalib/HandFist lines of
menu-settings.ini to go back to the built-in defaults.

IF YOU ADOPTED THE v0.5.0 BUNDLE: that vrpreset.ini shipped
effectsInFrame=1, which is what empties the health and EVE bars. Either
replace vrpreset.ini with this one, or just change that single line to
  effectsInFrame=0
and restart. Nothing else about your tuning is affected.

Your own files in
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\BioshockVR\
always win over the built-in defaults, key by key:

- Keep your tuning:        do nothing.
- Adopt this calibration:  back up and replace vrpreset.ini, hands.ini,
                           weapons.ini, menu-settings.ini and
                           BioshockVR.ini in %LOCALAPPDATA%\BioshockVR\
                           with these, then restart the game.
- Mix and match:           copy only the file you want (e.g. weapons.ini
                           for the per-weapon aim profiles), or even
                           single key=value lines between files - every
                           line stands alone.


IF THE GAME CRASHES OR MISBEHAVES: DELETE YOUR SETTINGS FIRST
=============================================================

Your saved settings live in

  %LOCALAPPDATA%\BioshockVR\

Paste that into the Explorer address bar (or press Win+R and paste it).
You should see vrpreset.ini, hands.ini, weapons.ini and bioshockvr.log.

Before reporting anything, try this - it fixes most odd behaviour and
costs you only your tuning:

  1. Close the game.
  2. Delete (or better, MOVE somewhere safe) vrpreset.ini, hands.ini
     and weapons.ini.
  3. Also delete command.txt if it is there - a leftover command file
     is re-applied at the next launch and can re-break things.
  4. Start the game and press VR PRESET 1.

Why it helps: your files override the mod's built-in defaults key by
key, so a value saved by an older version - or one saved while you were
mid-experiment - keeps applying even after an update fixes the default.
Deleting them puts you back on the shipped defaults, which are the ones
that get tested.

Keep bioshockvr.log. If the problem survives a clean settings folder,
that file is what makes it diagnosable - and crash dumps, if any, land
in %LOCALAPPDATA%\BioshockVR\crash\.

More help: TROUBLESHOOTING.txt at the zip root covers "no OpenXR
runtime / flat mode", SteamVR, broken API layers, and resolution setup.
